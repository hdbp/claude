---
name: histone-h1-weekly-digest
description: Weekly PubMed digest of new histone H1 / linker histone literature
---

Search PubMed for papers published in the last 7 days about histone H1 or linker histones. Use the PubMed MCP tool (mcp__plugin_bio-research_pubmed__search_articles) with the query: "histone H1"[Title/Abstract] OR "linker histone"[Title/Abstract] OR "H1 variant"[Title/Abstract] OR "H1.0"[Title/Abstract] OR "H1.1"[Title/Abstract] OR "H1.2"[Title/Abstract] OR "H1.3"[Title/Abstract] OR "H1.4"[Title/Abstract] OR "H1.5"[Title/Abstract]

Set date_from to 7 days ago (YYYY/MM/DD format) and sort by pub_date. Retrieve metadata for all results.

Then present a clean digest with:
- Total number of new papers found
- For each paper: Title (as a DOI link), authors (first + last only), journal, publication date, and a 1-2 sentence summary of the abstract focusing on what's new or notable about H1
- Group by sub-topic if there are enough papers (e.g., H1 variants, chromatin structure, H1 in disease, H1 in plants, H1 PTMs)
- End with a note of any H1 variant subtypes that appear multiple times this week

Always attribute results to PubMed and include DOI links per PubMed terms of use.
